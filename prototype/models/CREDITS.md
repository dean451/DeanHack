# Model credits

Every file here ships to players, so each one's license and credit line lives in this list.

## fox.glb

The fox's body and texture by PixelMannen, CC0 1.0: no credit required. Nothing else is in the
file: the skeleton, skin weights and clips that came with it (tomkranis, CC BY 4.0) were stripped
with `tools/strip-glb.mjs`, and the fox is rigged and animated by our own code (`fox-skin.js`, on
the cat's animator in `feline-skin.js`).

The body was taken from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets copy
(https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/Fox), whose glTF conversion
is credited there to @AsoboStudio and @scurest (CC BY 4.0). Swapping in PixelMannen's original
CC0 upload (OpenGameArt, "Fox and Shiba") would leave no conversion in the chain at all.
