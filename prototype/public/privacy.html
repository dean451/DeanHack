Privacy

DeanHack is a hobby project. It keeps only what the game needs to run, shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking, and sells nothing.

On the server

  • Your login. The login name you sign in with, or, on a guest login, a random id kept in a cookie (deanhack_guest, one year) so your browser finds its own game again.
  • Your game. Your characters' save files and the dungeon levels they are on, so you can come back to them.
  • The leaderboard. When a game ends, its character name, score, role, race, how it died and the date go on the public leaderboard, as in any NetHack game.
  • Log lines. A line when a game starts, ends, a character dies, or the dungeon is full, naming the login and the character. They are for keeping the server running, stay on the server and are never shared.
  • Backups. Saves and the leaderboard are backed up each night and kept for two weeks.

The server does not log which pages you load or your IP address. The connection to it is encrypted (HTTPS).

In your browser

A few settings are kept in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere: your character's name and class, the graphics quality, whether you have seen the keys card, and, if the game ever freezes, a short timing report for your own debugging. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Others involved

  • Google Fonts. The page's fonts are loaded from Google, which sees your IP address when it serves them. See Google's note on this.
  • Hosting. The server is a machine rented from Hetzner.

Deleting your data

To have your saves, leaderboard entries or log lines removed, or for any question about this page, ask on DeanHack's GitHub issues.
