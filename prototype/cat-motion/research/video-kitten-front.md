# Video notes: kitten walking toward the camera

**Source:** a stock-footage preview clip the user supplied (Envato, watermarked). It is 12.4 s long, 960×540, plays at 29.97 fps and is shot in slow motion at an unknown factor. The camera tracks the kitten from in front at ground level. Used for study only: no frames are stored in the repo.

## What the clip can give

The kitten walks straight at the camera, so only front-view measurements are usable. The hind legs are mostly hidden behind the body.

| Measurement | Value | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front-paw swing | ~21 frames (kitten's left fore: lifts ~frame 182/183, lands ~203–205) | Frame by frame, frames 170–229 |
| Front-paw stance | ≥ 52 frames (kitten's right fore stays planted from frame 170 through ~222) | Same pass |
| Front-leg duty factor | ≥ 0.71, so swing ≈ 0.29 of the cycle | Ratio from the two above; it holds whatever the slow-motion factor |
| Slow-motion factor | about 2.5–3.5× (*estimate*) | A swing of 21 frames, 0.70 s on playback, against a real 0.2–0.3 s swing |

The duty factor agrees with Trank 1996: 0.70 at a slow walk.

## Qualitative observations

- **Wrist flick in swing:** as the paw lifts, the wrist flexes hard and the paw curls under (frames ~184–191). It then unrolls and the leg reaches forward fully straight before touchdown (~193–203). This agrees with Caliebe 1991: fast wrist and knuckle flexion at lift-off.
- **Narrow track:** each swinging forepaw comes in toward the midline and lands almost under the chest, nearly in line with the other paw. The paws are not set at shoulder width.
- **Steady head:** across the walk the head holds its height and angle while the shoulders roll beneath it. The eyes stay level. This agrees with Zubair 2016.
- **Shoulders:** with each step the shoulder blade on the planted side rises and the chest rolls over the planted leg.
- **Tail:** carried upright with a slight curve. It sways only a little while walking.
- **Turning (from ~9 s):** the kitten heads round the wall in a smooth arc. It does not pivot in place.

## Can't be measured from this clip

- Joint angles: the view is from the front, not the side.
- Hind-leg timing.
- Absolute speed and stride rate: the slow-motion factor is unknown.
- Any sitting or lying.
